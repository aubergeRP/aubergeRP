## Your task

Write **one Pony-optimized positive prompt in English, approximately 70–110 words**.

Structure the prompt in this exact order:

1. **Pony quality tags**
   Always begin with:
   `score_9, score_8_up, score_7_up, score_6_up`
   Then add exactly one appropriate rating tag: `rating_safe`, `rating_questionable`, or `rating_explicit`.

2. **Main subject**
   Immediately describe the primary visible character: gender presentation when visually relevant, distinctive physical traits, hair, eyes, expression, clothing, body posture, pose, gaze direction, and current visible action.
   Prioritize stable appearance information from the Character section and current-state information from the Recent scene.

3. **Composition**
   Specify a useful framing or camera viewpoint when supported by the scene, such as `close-up`, `upper body`, `cowboy shot`, `full body`, `wide shot`, `from side`, `from behind`, `three-quarter view`, or `POV`.
   Choose framing that best communicates the current action rather than defaulting to a portrait.

4. **Environment**
   Describe where the scene is taking place, time of day when known, foreground/background elements, spatial relationships, and only the objects that materially help establish the scene.

5. **Lighting and atmosphere**
   Always specify the visible lighting source and quality.
   If the scene gives no lighting information, use plausible `soft diffused natural lighting`.
   Include atmosphere only when it can be represented visually.

6. **Art style**
   Always specify an explicit visual medium or style, such as `photograph`, `cinematic photograph`, `digital illustration`, `anime illustration`, `concept art`, or another style strongly implied by {raw_keywords}.
   Never leave the art style undefined.

7. **Booster tags**
   Finish with **3–8 short comma-separated tags** reinforcing the most important subject, composition, lighting, environment, or style concepts already described. Do not introduce new concepts here.

* Preserve clear spatial relationships between characters and important objects.
* When several actions occurred, depict only the **latest visually observable moment**.
* Do not combine incompatible moments from different exchanges.

## Content rules

* **Write in English only.** Translate scene descriptions, objects, actions, locations, and generic titles into English.
* Keep proper character names unchanged.
* Describe only visually observable information.
* Do not include dialogue, quotations, thoughts, motivations, memories, personality traits, backstory, smells, sounds, or other non-visual information.
